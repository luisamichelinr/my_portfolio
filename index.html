My floating lanterns

I was born in Campinas, but I moved around a lot; in fact, I never attended the same school for more than a year until I was 9 years old
Growing up, I was surrounded by strong women, like my mother and my godmother, who inspired me to dream without limits
I always spent my vacations and weekends in Birigui, a countryside town in São Paulo, Brazil, with my mother's family, so I grew up with my brother and cousins
Even though he passed away young due to cancer, my grandfather showed me that caring for others isn't about grand gestures, but about the little things in everyday life
School has always been my second home, even though it changed several times before it got a name: SESI, the place where I discovered my love for technology
