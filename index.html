My floating lanterns

Luliês

When I was little, I mispronounced so many words that my family called my way of speaking Luliês, name inspired by my nickname, Luli. My mom was usually the only one who could understand what I was trying to say.

I went to speech therapy when I was approximately two to four years old.

The Place Where I Stayed

When I was nine, I placed fifth in the SESI entrance exam after missing one question. And it became the first school where I studied for more than a year.

There, between a lot of classes, I found Run Marco and got fascinated by programming and robotics.

FIRST LEGO League

Hally Team Robotics Team

FIRST LEGO League (FLL) is an international robotics competition where students design and program LEGO robots to complete missions and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems. Each season brings a new theme, combining research, technology, and teamwork.

I joined Hally Team in September 2021 and, over the next three years, I explored different sides of robotics, including building, programming, researching, presentations, social campaigns, and activities to share the values of robotics with other students at our school.

In 2024, I also had the opportunity to mentor another team preparing for their own competition.

Tocando em Frente

Tocando em Frente (TEF) is a Brazilian youth-led nonprofit organization that works to expand educational opportunities for public school students. Through volunteer-led projects, workshops, and educational materials, we help students explore new interests and discover opportunities they might not otherwise have access to.
